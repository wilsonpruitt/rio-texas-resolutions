## Walkthrough 1: Submit a Resolution (as a Petitioner)

1. Go to https://rio-texas-resolutions.vercel.app/en/submit
2. Log in with **delegate@riotexas.org**
3. Walk through the 4-step submission form:
   - **Step 1** — Info (what the process looks like)
   - **Step 2** — Account (already logged in, so this is skipped)
   - **Step 3** — Write your resolution (title, text, rationale — English and/or Spanish)
   - **Step 4** — Review and submit
4. After submitting, you'll see a display number assigned (e.g., RT-2026-0013)
5. Notice: if your resolution mentions money, budgets, or funding, the system automatically flags it for finance committee referral under SR-14

## Walkthrough 2: Review the Dashboard (as Admin / Asst. to Bishop)

1. Log out, then log in with **assistant@riotexas.org**
2. Go to **Dashboard** — you'll see all 12 demo resolutions sorted by status, with counts at the top
3. Click into any resolution to see the full detail view:
   - **Pipeline tracker** — visual bar showing where the resolution sits in the process
   - **Bilingual content** — toggle between English and Spanish in the top nav
   - **Committee assignment** — assign a resolution to a Vision Team or Admin Agency
   - **Financial flag** — yellow warning if the system detected budget implications
4. From here you can advance resolutions through each stage

## Walkthrough 3: Committee Review (as Committee Chair)

1. Log out, then log in with **chair-csr@riotexas.org**
2. Go to **Dashboard** — you'll see only resolutions assigned to your committee (CSR)
3. Open a resolution that's Under Review and submit a recommendation (Recommended or Not Recommended, with explanation)

## Walkthrough 4: Final Decision (as Secretary)

1. Log out, then log in with **secretary@riotexas.org**
2. Open a resolution in UT Review status
3. Record the final UT decision: Approved or Not Advanced

## Walkthrough 5: Try it in Spanish

1. Click the language toggle in the top navigation bar
2. The entire interface switches to Spanish — all labels, status names, and navigation
3. Resolution content appears in Spanish when available (falls back to English if not)

## Key Features

- **Bilingual** — full English/Spanish UI and content fields
- **Financial detection** — automatically flags resolutions with budget implications and cites SR-14
- **Pipeline tracking** — visual status tracker from Draft through Approved/Not Advanced
- **Role-based views** — each role sees only what they need
